## Hi! I'm Haruka 👋

I'm a Ph.D. student in [Prof. Matt Sigman's lab](https://www.sigmanlab.com) at the University of Utah.
My research combines data science, cheminformatics, and computational chemistry to gain mechanistic insight into and predict reactivity in Ni catalysis. I also work at the bench to bridge both computational and experimental chemistry. I work with Claude to code.
